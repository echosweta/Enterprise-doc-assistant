# Vendor Approval SOP

## Review thresholds
Purchases above 10,000 USD require a procurement review and a vendor security assessment before signing. Purchases above 25,000 USD also require three competitive quotes.

## Contract signing
Only authorised signatories may sign vendor contracts. Employees must never sign contracts on behalf of the company.
