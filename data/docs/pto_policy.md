# Paid Time Off Policy

## Annual entitlement
Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid time off (PTO) per calendar year. PTO accrues monthly at 2 days per month. Part-time employees accrue PTO in proportion to their contracted hours.

## Carry over
Employees may carry over a maximum of 5 unused PTO days into the next year. Any days above 5 expire on 31 March.

## Requesting leave
Requests longer than 3 consecutive days must be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance through the HR portal and approved by your manager. Shorter requests need 2 working days notice.

## Sick leave
Sick leave is separate from PTO. Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year. A medical certificate is required after 3 consecutive sick days.
