# Travel and Expense Policy

## Meals
Meal expenses are reimbursed up to 50 USD per day while travelling. Alcohol is not reimbursable.

## Submission deadline
Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the expense. Late claims need approval from the finance director.

## Approvals
Any single expense above 500 USD requires prior written approval from your manager.

## Flights and hotels
Book economy class for flights shorter than 6 hours. Business class may be booked for flights longer than 6 hours with VP approval. Hotels are capped at 200 USD per night.
