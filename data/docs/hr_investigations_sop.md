# HR Investigations SOP

## Timeline
Formal workplace investigations must be completed within 30 days of the complaint. The investigator must be independent of the reporting line of the people involved.

## Confidentiality
Case files are restricted to the HR investigations team. Outcomes are shared only with the employee concerned and their manager.
