# VPN Setup and Troubleshooting Guide

## Installation
Download the GlobalConnect client from the Software Center. Open GlobalConnect and set the server address to vpn.acme.example. Sign in with your company email and approve the multi-factor authentication (MFA) prompt on your phone.

## Troubleshooting
If you see error 812, your MFA token is out of sync. Open the self-service portal, choose Reset MFA, and re-enrol your phone. If the VPN connects but internal sites do not load, disconnect and reconnect to refresh the DNS settings.

## Contacting support
If the problem persists, raise an IT ticket from the portal or call the helpdesk on extension 4400.
