# Laptop Request and Refresh SOP

## Requesting a laptop
Submit a hardware request in the IT portal. Your manager must approve it. Approved laptops are delivered within 5 business days.

## Refresh cycle
Company laptops are refreshed every 3 years. Early replacement is possible only if the device is damaged or fails a hardware diagnostic.

## Lost or stolen devices
Report a lost or stolen laptop to the security team within 4 hours so the device can be remotely wiped.
