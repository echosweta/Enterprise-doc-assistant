# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

## Office attendance
Employees follow a hybrid schedule with a minimum of 3 days per week in the office. Teams choose their anchor days, typically Tuesday to Thursday.

## Working from another location
Employees may work remotely from a different city or country for up to 30 days per calendar year with manager approval. Working abroad requires tax and legal clearance from HR at least 4 weeks in advance.

## Home office stipend
New employees receive a one-time home office stipend of 500 USD to purchase a desk, chair or monitor. Receipts must be submitted within 60 days of joining.
