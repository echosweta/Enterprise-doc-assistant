# New Employee Onboarding SOP

## First day
On day one new hires receive their laptop, badge and accounts. IT provides a setup session at 10 AM. Each new hire is assigned an onboarding buddy.

## Mandatory training
Security awareness and code of conduct training must be completed within 14 days of joining.

## 30-60-90 plan
Managers set a 30-60-90 day plan in the first week and hold check-ins at the end of each period.
