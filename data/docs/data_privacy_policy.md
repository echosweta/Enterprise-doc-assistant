# Data Privacy and Classification Policy

## Data classification
Company data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. Personal data about customers or employees is Confidential by default.

## Handling personal data
Personally identifiable information (PII) must be encrypted at rest and in transit. Never share PII through personal email or messaging apps.

## Breach reporting
Suspected data breaches must be reported to the privacy team at privacy@acme.example within 24 hours of discovery.

## Retention
Financial records are retained for 7 years. Customer support records are retained for 3 years, after which they are deleted.
