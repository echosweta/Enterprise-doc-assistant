# Security Incident Reporting SOP

## Phishing emails
Report suspicious emails using the Report Phishing button in your mail client. Do not click links or open attachments.

## Severity levels
A P1 incident, such as confirmed data loss or ransomware, must be reported to the security hotline within 15 minutes. P2 incidents must be reported within 4 hours.

## After reporting
The security team will acknowledge your report within 30 minutes for P1 incidents and will contact you if more information is needed.
