# Parental Leave Policy

## Eligibility
All employees with at least 6 months of service are eligible for parental leave.

## Duration
The primary caregiver receives 16 weeks of fully paid leave. The secondary caregiver receives 6 weeks of fully paid leave. Leave may be taken within 12 months of the birth or adoption.

## How to apply
Notify HR at least 30 days before the expected start date by submitting the parental leave form in the HR portal.
