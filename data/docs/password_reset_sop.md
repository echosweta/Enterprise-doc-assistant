# Password Reset SOP

## Self-service reset
Go to the self-service portal and choose Forgot password. Verify your identity with MFA and set a new password.

## Password rules
Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and include upper case, lower case, a number and a symbol. Passwords expire every 90 days. You cannot reuse your last 12 passwords.

## Account lockout
Your account is locked after 5 failed sign-in attempts. Locked accounts unlock automatically after 30 minutes, or you can call the helpdesk on extension 4400 for an immediate unlock.
